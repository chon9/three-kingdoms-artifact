## Artwork

The Three Kingdoms card art in `assets/art/` (`assets/art/cards/<card id>.jpg`)
is original to this project. It was generated with OpenAI's built-in image
tool from the prompts in [docs/art/ART_PROMPTS.md](docs/art/ART_PROMPTS.md)
and [docs/art/art_prompts.csv](docs/art/art_prompts.csv); the final prompt and
reference role of each image are recorded in
[docs/art/generated_art_manifest.json](docs/art/generated_art_manifest.json),
and review status in [docs/art/GENERATION_STATUS.md](docs/art/GENERATION_STATUS.md).
It is distributed with the repository under the project's [MIT license](LICENSE).
The committed files are 512x768 JPEG reductions made by `tools/optimize_art.py`;
the 1024x1536 masters are kept outside the repository.

## Card text and names

The Three Kingdoms names and rules text in `data/locale/` are original to this
project. Original Artifact Classic card names and rules text (the optional
"classic" skin) are Valve's and are used for compatibility reference only.
