# Artifact Classic mechanics taxonomy

Generated by `mechanics.py` from Valve's final card data (set 00 + set 01), Valve localization glossary tags, the Valve glossary and the project's catalog. Do not edit by hand; edit the script.

**Scope:** 303 cards (Creep 43, Hero 48, Improvement 39, Item 48, Spell 118, Token 7) plus 75 hero/creep/item/improvement abilities listed separately (ability mechanics are folded into their parent card for the frequency tables). Implemented in the engine today: 231; mapped but unimplemented: 72.

Per-card fields in `mechanics.json`: id, name, type, colour, costs, stats, text, clauses[] (each with trigger, trigger_filter, targets, scope, verbs, stats, durations, conditions, random, granted, source), aggregated triggers/targets/verbs/durations/keywords/conditions, cross_lane, pulses, complexity (1-5) with reasons, hard_reason, and engine {implemented, full, partial, missing, coverage}.

## Rules questions (ambiguous in glossary / wiki)

**RQ-01. Does 'each enemy' (Tower Barrage, Thunderstorm, Conflagration, Ignite, Echo Slam) include the enemy tower?**

- Best answer: No: 'enemy' means enemy units. March of the Machines says 'deal 2 damage to the enemy tower and 2 damage to each enemy', which would be redundant if towers were enemies; Echo Slam would count the tower. Engine currently adds the tower for Tower Barrage/Thunderstorm (include_tower) - recommend removing unless a reference match shows otherwise.
- Source: Valve loc CardText_10291 (March of the Machines), CardText_10323 (Echo Slam); glossary DCG_Glossary_Unit ('Towers are not units'). Confidence: medium-high.

**RQ-02. Who gets bounty when a unit dies to its owner's own effect (Annihilation, Lich Sacrifice, Bracers of Sacrifice, At Any Cost)?**

- Best answer: The opponent of the dying unit's owner, always. The glossary defines Bounty as gold 'awarded to the opponent when this unit dies' with no exception. (A community glossary claims no gold for condemning your own cards; it is unsourced.)
- Source: valve_glossary.txt DCG_Glossary_Bounty, DCG_Glossary_Creep; docs/RULES.md (engine already does this); counter-claim: .tools/artifact_compiled.json 'Condemn'. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-03. Lane scope of 'after you play ...' triggers (Zeus, Ogre Magi, Outworld Devourer, Path of the X, Tresdin's Standards, Coordinated Assault, Heroic Resolve...).**

- Best answer: Only cards played while the trigger source is in the active lane (an improvement: its own lane). Storm Spirit explicitly says 'in any lane' and Crystal Maiden 'in each lane', implying the default is the source's lane. Engine Static Field already scans the active lane only.
- Source: Wiki Phases ('cards and abilities generally only affect the active lane, unless otherwise noted'); Valve text 10537, 10518. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-04. Default scope of 'allies', 'enemies', 'all units', 'all heroes', 'enemy heroes', 'allied heroes' without a lane qualifier.**

- Best answer: The active lane (for spells) or the source's lane (continuous / triggered). Only texts that say 'in all lanes', 'in any lane', 'every lane' or name the Fountain reach elsewhere (Drow, Thundergod's Wrath, Diabolic Revelation, Whispers of Madness, Rolling Storm, Routed).
- Source: Wiki Phases; contrast Drow 'Other allies in all lanes' vs Disciple of Nevermore 'Other allies'. Confidence: medium-high.

**RQ-05. Who are a unit's 'attackers' (Timbersaw, Keenfolk Plate)?**

- Best answer: Enemy units in the lane whose current combat target is that unit (not cleave sources, not spell battles). Recomputed whenever combat targets change (taunt, redirect, death, placement).
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Battle / Unblocked (combat target); patch 2018-12-20 Reactive Armor text. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-06. When does Retaliate apply?**

- Best answer: Whenever the unit is attacked during ANY battle (combat phase, Duel, Gank, Friendly Fire, Berserker's Call, Winter's Curse), once per attacker, even if the retaliator is stunned or disarmed. It is reduced by the attacker's armor (armor reduces each damage source). Cleave and spell damage do not trigger it.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Retaliate ('even outside of the combat phase'), DCG_Glossary_Stunned, DCG_Glossary_Disarmed, DCG_Glossary_Armor. Confidence: high (armor reduction: medium).

**RQ-07. Do Siege / Cleave apply when the unit is disarmed, stunned, or attacking the tower?**

- Best answer: No. Both are 'when blocked during the combat phase, in addition to battle damage'; a disarmed unit does not attack; an unblocked unit (combat target = tower) deals normal damage to the tower and nothing extra. Siege counts for diagonal targets (combat target is a unit, so it is blocked). Tower armor (Steel Reinforcement) reduces siege.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Siege, _Cleave, _Unblocked, _Disarm. Confidence: high (tower armor: medium).

**RQ-08. Cleave: which units, and is it armor-reduced / piercing?**

- Best answer: The blocking unit's (target's) allied neighbors (left/right in the target's row). Each cleave hit is reduced by that unit's armor; a unit with Pierce makes it piercing because cleave is battle damage.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Cleave, _BattleDamage, _BattleDamageIsPiercing. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-09. Regeneration timing and cap.**

- Best answer: Heals during the combat phase simultaneously with combat damage and is applied before the death check (can save a unit), capped at max health; applies even if the unit is not attacked. Tower regeneration (Path of the Dreamer) heals the tower in combat.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Regeneration; wiki Phases ('Health Regeneration resolves at the same time as combat damage'). Confidence: high (cap: medium).

**RQ-10. Damage Immunity vs condemn / lethal / Death Shield.**

- Best answer: Immune units take no damage from any source but still die to condemn (Lethal, Annihilation, Slay). A Death Shield saves from condemn as well as damage. Lethal effects ('condemn each unit it deals battle damage to') need damage actually dealt, so an immune unit is not condemned by them.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_DamageImmunity, _Condemn, _DeathShield, _LethalToCreeps. Confidence: high (lethal vs immunity: medium).

**RQ-11. Death Shield stacking and duration.**

- Best answer: A unit holds at most one; giving another is wasted. When it would die it removes the shield and survives at 1 health. 'this round' shields (Cheating Death) expire at end of round; play-effect shields (Relentless Zombie) last until used.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_DeathShield; patch 2018-12-20 Cheating Death text. Confidence: high.

**RQ-12. What does 'Purge your opponent's effects' remove?**

- Best answer: Modifications and temporary effects whose source is the opponent (stun/silence/disarm, -armor modifies, Viper Strike, Track, Caught Unprepared), not damage, not base abilities, not equipped items, not continuous auras from neighbors/improvements. Requires modifier provenance (source player) on every effect.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Purge; patch 2018-12-20 Jasper Daggers. Confidence: high.

**RQ-13. What survives the Fountain?**

- Best answer: Equipped items and Modify effects survive; temporary effects and 'until it dies' effects (Viper Strike, Track, Caught Unprepared) are removed; the hero is fully healed. Charges/hacks on hand cards are unaffected.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Fountain, _Modify; wiki Fountains. Confidence: high.

**RQ-14. Lock details.**

- Best answer: A locked card cannot be played; at end of round every locked card loses 1 Lock; Lock stacks additively. Lost in Time / Buying Time pick distinct random cards in hand (fewer if the hand is smaller). Hourglass locks the drawn card itself.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Lock, _Locked. Confidence: high (distinct picks: medium).

**RQ-15. Pulse semantics inside multi-step effects.**

- Best answer: At each Pulse continuous effects update, reactive abilities process, and condemned / lethally damaged units are destroyed (deaths, bounty, pathing reset). Random choices after a Pulse exclude units that just died. Valve marks Pulses in the localization (g:608) for Poof, Chain Frost, Eclipse, Wrath of Gold, etc.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_ZToken; Valve loc [g:608] markers (pulse counts in mechanics.json). Confidence: high.

**RQ-16. Chain Frost bounce target.**

- Best answer: 'its' is the unit damaged by the previous hit; candidates are that unit's existing allied neighbors (same row). Best guess: if it has no neighbor (or both died), the remaining bounces fizzle. Needs a reference match.
- Source: Valve text 10318; glossary Allied Neighbors. Confidence: low.

**RQ-17. Where do summoned / moved / stolen units go?**

- Best answer: Summon: a random empty combat position on the owner's side of the named lane (glossary). Moves to another lane and stolen creeps: treat like a summon (random empty position, then roll its arrow). Cursed Satyr's Zombie goes to the opponent's side of Satyr's lane.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Summon; wiki Pathing ('Any new unblocked unit ... will have a Pathing Card placed'). Confidence: high for summon, low for moves/control.

**RQ-18. Taunt scope and duration.**

- Best answer: All of the taunting unit's enemy neighbors (three positions across) set their combat target to it; units elsewhere are unaffected. It is a combat-target change, so it lasts until the next re-target or the end of the round like Battlefield Control.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Taunt; artifact_compiled 'Taunt' (does not affect cards that cannot normally attack the unit). Confidence: medium (duration).

**RQ-19. Legal choices for 'Choose a combat target' (Battlefield Control, New Orders, Compel, Murder Plot, Assassin's Veil...).**

- Best answer: Any enemy unit in the three positions across; the tower only if the position directly across is empty (Forward Charge: 'the unit or tower across from them'; pathing: an empty diagonal means forward). Engine currently allows the tower when either diagonal is empty.
- Source: Valve text 10413; wiki Pathing; engine combat_targets(). Confidence: medium.

**RQ-20. Initiative after Enough Magic! (proceed to combat without passes).**

- Best answer: Not specified. Best guess: the caster's opponent gets initiative for the next lane (same as any action without Get Initiative: engine _finish_action(player, false)). Needs a reference match.
- Source: Valve text 10326; glossary Get Initiative. Confidence: low.

**RQ-21. Glyph of Confusion: what counts as 'enters this lane'?**

- Best answer: Every placement into the lane: deployment of heroes and lane creeps, creep cards, summons, moves from other lanes, units whose control changes. Not swaps within the lane. 'this round' means the round in which it entered (deployment entrants are stunned for the whole new round).
- Source: Valve text 10187; glossary Stun. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-22. Kanna: when and where do the random allied Melee Creeps go?**

- Best answer: If Kanna is in play (already in a lane or being deployed this phase), both of her player's lane creeps enter her lane after deployment is revealed. The public pending-creep reveal must therefore show them as 'to Kanna's lane' (her destination stays private until reveal). If Kanna is in the Fountain, creeps use their random lanes.
- Source: Valve text 10503; wiki Phases (creeps randomly assigned before hero choice). Confidence: medium.

**RQ-23. Order of deployment-phase summons (Venomancer, Prellex, Barracks).**

- Best answer: They resolve at deployment reveal together with lane creeps, into the source's lane after heroes are placed (a hero deployed this round summons into its new lane). A hero in the Fountain summons nothing.
- Source: Valve text 10491, 10523, 10147. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-24. Do active abilities start ready? (heroes / items / improvements)**

- Best answer: Improvements: ready as soon as played (wiki Improvements). Heroes: wiki says the ability 'starts on a cooldown that begins to count down from the start of the game' and ticks down at end of every round even in the Fountain; engine starts at 0 (ready). Items: assume ready when equipped. Keep as a configurable rule until a reference match settles it.
- Source: Wiki Heroes/Improvements; docs/RULES.md (J'Muy cooldown open). Confidence: low for heroes.

**RQ-25. Quicken.**

- Best answer: After using Finger of Death its cooldown is set to 4, then reduced by 1 (not below 1) - effectively 3.
- Source: Valve text 10449; patch notes 2018-12-20 ('reduce cooldown by 1, but not below 1'). Confidence: high.

**RQ-26. Shop Deed with several deeds / negative prices.**

- Best answer: Each deed reduces Secret Shop prices by the item's base cost; a second deed makes prices negative and buying pays you gold.
- Source: Richard Garfield Goes Shopping (Steam news 2018-11-15). Confidence: high.

**RQ-27. Starting charges of March of the Machines / Bitter Enemies.**

- Best answer: March of the Machines starts with 3 charges. Bitter Enemies' starting count is not in Valve's data; best guess 3 (same design pattern). Must be a data field (charges_initial).
- Source: Richard Garfield on Upkeep Kills (Steam news 2018-11-08) for March; none for Bitter Enemies. Confidence: high / low.

**RQ-28. Rounding of 'half' (Sven, Revtel Convoy, Mercenary Exiles, Routed, Wingfall Hammer) and of 'evenly divided' (Mystic Flare).**

- Best answer: Round down. For Mystic Flare 12 split among N targets: floor(12/N) each, remainder lost (best guess). Needs reference.
- Source: Valve texts; no source. Confidence: low.

**RQ-29. Summoned Meepo (Divided We Stand): hero or creep? What happens when it dies?**

- Best answer: It is a hero unit (hero colour, 5 bounty, can equip items, Soulbound links it to other Meepos). Decided in M4: like any hero it returns to the Fountain when killed and is redeployed later, so a summoned copy joins its owner's roster (SUMMONED_HEROES_JOIN_ROSTER). The earlier best guess (not in the roster, gone when it dies) had no source. Needs a reference match.
- Source: Valve text 10004, 10005; glossary Hero ('When it is killed, it ... returns to the Fountain to be redeployed later'), Soulbound. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-30. Swaps and pathing.**

- Best answer: Arrows stay with the combat positions when units swap (Cunning Plan, Juke, Rebel Decoy, Phase Boots); the unit now in a position inherits that position's arrow. A chosen combat target (Battlefield Control, Compel, a taunt) is an arrow like any other: the unit swapped into the position inherits it, and an enemy forced at a swapped unit keeps its arrow and attacks the unit now in that position (M4 review).
- Source: Wiki Pathing 'Resetting Pathing'. Confidence: high.

**RQ-31. Silence vs passive abilities and colour.**

- Best answer: Silence stops active abilities (unit and item) and colour contribution, not passives/continuous effects. Stun = silence + disarm. Stunned/disarmed units still retaliate.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Silence, _Stun, _Stunned. Confidence: high.

**RQ-32. 'When X deals battle damage to a unit' (Ursa, Viper, Hellbear Crippler, Loyal Beast) with 0 damage after armor.**

- Best answer: Does not trigger: damage must actually be dealt (> 0 after armor, not prevented by immunity).
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_BattleDamage. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-33. Cross Lane cards.**

- Best answer: Use mana and a hero of the card's colour from the active lane, but the target / destination may be any lane. All improvements are cross lane. 'in all lanes' effects are global but NOT cross lane (still need the coloured hero in the active lane). Decided in M5 (RQ-55, medium confidence): a unit the text names without a lane (Gank's, Ball Lightning's and The Cover of Night's allied black hero) is in the active lane; only the part written 'in any lane' or 'another lane' reaches other lanes.
- Source: Glossary DCG_Glossary_Inspect_Crosslane, DCG_Glossary_Improvement. Confidence: high.

**RQ-34. Improvements per lane.**

- Best answer: Each lane holds at most 16 improvements; a 17th destroys a random existing one. Improvements are not units and occupy no combat position.
- Source: Wiki Improvements. Confidence: medium.

**RQ-35. Caught Unprepared duration.**

- Best answer: The stun stays until that hero equips an item, including across rounds; best guess: it is a temporary (non-modify) effect, so the Fountain removes it.
- Source: Valve text 10396; wiki Fountains (lists Caught Unprepared as removed). Confidence: medium.

**RQ-36. Hacked cards (Self Sabotage) and Multicast/...And One For Me copies.**

- Best answer: A 'base copy' is the unmodified card (no locks, charges, hacks). A hacked card keeps its hack until played; the extra text resolves when it is played by its owner (damaging the owner's random tower).
- Source: Valve texts 10302, 10509, 10196; glossary DCG_Glossary_NumHacks ('The text of this card has been changed'). Confidence: medium.

## Implemented cards: divergences from Valve final text

- **Battlefield Control (4010)**: Battlefield Control: engine offers the tower when ANY of the three positions across is empty; see RQ-19 (tower should need the position directly across to be empty).
- **Berserker's Call (10288)**: Berserker's Call: matches; retaliate/cleave/pierce are not applied in _battle_neighbors yet.
- **Coup de Grace (10308)**: Coup de Grace: matches (discard random, then condemn a hero); no death-shield interaction yet.
- **Annihilation (10315)**: Annihilation: Valve 'Condemn all units.'; engine condemns the active lane only (consistent with lane scope RQ-04), and pays bounty for every unit (RQ-02).
- **Tower Barrage (10402)**: Tower Barrage: Valve final text is 'Deal 2 damage to each enemy.'; classic_cards.json sets include_tower=true (also damages the enemy tower). See rules question RQ-01.
- **Thunderstorm (10412)**: Thunderstorm: Valve final text is 'Deal 4 damage to each enemy.'; classic_cards.json sets include_tower=true. See RQ-01.
- **Forward Charge (10413)**: Forward Charge: engine sets arrow 0 and combat_target -1 for every ally (straight ahead); Valve says 'change their combat target to the unit or tower across'. Equivalent unless a Taunt/forced target is later applied.

## Design notes for the effect system

- Every reactive trigger in Valve's glossary is 'simultaneous' (before action / after combat): collect all triggered effects, order them deterministically (e.g. initiative player first, then lane position left to right, then uid), and resolve with Pulses between.
- Card instances need state in hand and deck (Lock, Eclipse charges, Self Sabotage hacks): hands must hold instance dictionaries {cid, card, lock, charges, hacks} instead of bare ids. player_view must redact the opponent's instances except public counters (lock is visible on the card back: DCG 'enemyCardLocked' effect).
- Modifiers need provenance {source_uid, source_player, kind: modify|temp|until_death|until_next_combat|continuous} so Purge, Fountain and end-of-round cleanup are exact.
- Combat must become per-attacker events (battle record: attacker, target, damage after armor, piercing) so retaliate, cleave, lethal, 'when X deals battle damage', Rebel Instigator/Red Mist Pillager conditions and Unearthed Secrets can be evaluated deterministically.
- Improvements and towers need their own state per lane (improvements list, tower armor/retaliate/regeneration/mana modifiers).
- Random choices must all go through the seeded engine rng in a fixed order (iterate lanes 0..2, positions left to right, player 0 then 1) to keep replays and Steam host authority stable; hidden-information randomness (opponent hand picks) must never be resolved on the guest.
