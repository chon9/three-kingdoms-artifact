# Rule coverage and fidelity

The target is **Artifact Classic (the first version)**. Foundry's unified mana, shared action phase and fixed five-slot lanes are not the target.

This file separates implemented mechanics from verified fidelity. The supported catalog contains all 303 counted Classic definitions with preserved Valve artwork: the 49 migrated in M1; from M3, the 92 red and green cards plus three heroes' signature improvements (Steel Reinforcement, Nether Ward, Verdant Refuge); from M4, the 44 blue cards plus two more heroes' signature improvements (Barracks, Ignite); from M5, the 39 black cards plus two more heroes' signature improvements (Assault Ladders, March of the Machines); from M6, the 40 remaining items (every Classic weapon, armor, accessory, consumable and Deed); from M7, the 32 remaining improvements, so all 39 Classic improvements ship. Card effects use Valve's localization/glossary, with Classic balance-patch corrections for Axe, Gust and equipment prices. Automated tests cover supported effects, twenty-two presets, every matchup of the original three and bot matches with each M3 to M7 preset. Exact Classic behavior still needs reference matches around deployment, combat targeting restrictions and unusual combinations.

Card rules are data: every card entry in `data/classic_cards.json` (schema 4) and `data/training_cards.json` (schema 2) carries its rules, which the rules engine in `scripts/core/rules/` runs (see "Rules engine" below and `docs/EFFECTS.md`). Open rules questions are numbered RQ-xx and listed under "Rules questions".

## Combat positions

Each lane holds one ordered list of combat positions. In the engine this is stored as `lanes[i].units[0]` and `lanes[i].units[1]`. These two rows always have the same length, and index `n` in both is the same position. "Neighbors" are the own-row units at `n - 1` and `n + 1`, and an empty slot means no neighbor on that side. "Enemy neighbors" are the enemy-row units at `n - 1`, `n` and `n + 1`. Static Field, Berserker's Call, Gust and arrows all read this aligned model.

A unit's legal combat targets (`combat_targets`, used by Battlefield Control) are its enemy neighbors, plus the enemy tower only when the position directly across (`n` in the enemy row) is empty (RQ-19). An empty diagonal alone does not open the tower, and a unit that later enters the position directly across ends a forced tower target.

Commands:

- `{"type": "play", "hand": i, "position": p}` plays a creep card. `p` is the index of an existing position whose own slot is empty, `-1` for a new position at the left end, or the current position count for a new position at the right end. An empty lane offers only `0`. `legal_actions` lists one action per choice.
- `{"type": "hold"}` is legal in the shop when the player has at least 1 gold, the Secret Shop slot is not sold, and nothing has been held yet this phase.

State:

- `state.pending_creeps` holds this round's lane-creep lanes, as `[[lane, lane], [lane, lane]]`. It is public and is cleared after placement.
- `players[p].held_secret` is the held Secret Shop card. It is hidden from the opponent.
- `state.event_seq` counts every event ever emitted.

Arrows are relative offsets. A new left-end position shifts both rows together, so no attacker changes target. Compaction removes only positions that are empty on both sides, so an attacker and its diagonal target stay adjacent.

## Rules engine

Card rules are declarative JSON inside each card entry, validated at load against a closed vocabulary. The contributor guide is `docs/EFFECTS.md`; the full design is `docs/design/EFFECT_SYSTEM_SPEC.md`.

- **Modules.** `scripts/core/rules/`: `vocab.gd` (the vocabulary), `rules_db.gd` (compiler and validator), `select.gd` (selectors and canonical order), `stats.gd` (derived stats, auras, settle), `vm.gd` (ops, expressions, conditions), `kernel.gd` (randomness, event bus, trigger queue, the Pulse, damage, modifiers), `combat.gd`, `zones.gd`, `targeting.gd` (`legal_actions`), and `natives.gd` (empty). `match_engine.gd` keeps the public API and the phase flow.
- **The Pulse.** Settle continuous effects, collect dying units (0 health or condemned), consume Death Shields, close Soulbound from the units that still die (its added partners may use their own shields), snapshot and remove the dead (bounty, Fountain, compaction), emit `death` and `unit_died`, then resolve the queued triggers as one batch. It repeats until nothing is queued and nothing died. Guards: 2048 ops per command, 16 nested Pulses, 64 loops per Pulse; overflow stops resolution deterministically with a `rules_overflow` event.
- **Modifiers.** A permanent Modify is written into the unit's fields and recorded with its source and player, so Purge can reverse the opponent's (RQ-12). Temporary effects (`give`) are read at read time and expire by duration: `round`, `next_combat`, `death`, `equip`, `used`.
- **Determinism.** Randomness only through `Kernel.rand_index` and `Kernel.chance`; candidates in one canonical order (player, lane, slot); integers only. Read-only functions (`legal_actions`, `combat_preview`, `player_view`, the `*_of` helpers) never change state or randomness.
- **Versions.** `RULES_VERSION` (`scripts/core/rules/rules_version.gd`, generated by `python tools/rules_hash.py`) hashes the rules code and both card data packs. It is recorded in replays and is part of the Steam content hash, so peers and replays with different rules refuse each other. `tests/rules_schema_test.gd` fails when it is stale.
- **Zone and hand operations.** `move`, `swap`, `control`, `lock`, `add_card`, `card_counter` and hacks (a `modify` with a hack grant aimed at cards in hand) are implemented (M2) and exercised by the rules fixture pack (`tests/fixtures/rules_fixtures.json`), the keyword matrix and RQ cases in `tests/mechanics_test.gd`, and `tests/fuzz_test.gd`.

## Assumptions pending verification

- **Compaction timing.** A position that is empty on both sides is removed as soon as units leave the lane. That covers death cleanup after spells, Static Field, Berserker's Call and combat, and a unit returned to the Fountain. Units killed together keep their positions until all of them are removed. No source states Classic's exact collapse timing.
- **Random placement.** Summons, lane creeps and deployed heroes prefer a random empty own slot, and open a new position at a random end only when none exists. As a result, round 1 always starts with every lane fully blocked (two positions, 2 v 2).
- **Fair reveal order.** Units placed at reveal (both players' heroes and creeps) are placed in one RNG-shuffled order. Classic's real interleaving is unknown.
- **Changing plans.** Until a player confirms deployment, a planned hero can be moved to another lane or taken back. Classic was not recorded doing either; both are private and draw no randomness.
- **Forced targets.** A chosen combat target (Battlefield Control, Compel, a taunt) is treated like a rolled arrow. A unit placed directly across from a unit attacking diagonally resets it to attack the new unit, whether its target was rolled or chosen (wiki Pathing, "Resetting Pathing"; before the M4 review a chosen unit target was kept). A chosen target is also reset when the chosen unit leaves play, stays with the position when units swap (RQ-30), and ends at the next deployment. A unit sent at the tower is blocked the same way: the tower is a combat target only while the position directly across is empty (RQ-19), so a unit that enters that position becomes its target (`Zones.place`; `rq_19_tower_needs_empty_across`; the Compel and Ventriloquy "pathing reset" spec cases).
- **Holding then buying.** Buying a held Secret Shop item in the same phase cancels the hold. The gold already paid is not refunded.
- **Ancient overflow** and the **J'Muy cooldown** timing remain as previously documented.
- **Zone edge cases (M2).** A move to a position that was taken in the meantime fizzles; a control change of a roster hero fizzles (only creeps and summoned heroes change sides); lane creeps that follow a hero (RQ-22) go to lane 0 when no such hero can be found at the reveal; locks apply only to cards in hand.
- **Low-confidence rules defaults** (RQ-16, RQ-20, RQ-22, RQ-24, RQ-28, RQ-29, RQ-37, RQ-49 to RQ-58, RQ-60) each sit behind one constant or data entry, so a reference match can flip them without code changes. RQ-44's newer cases, Incarnation of Selemene and the four Paths seeing their own play (medium-low confidence), would need a `card_played` filter that excludes the source to flip. The Omexe Arena's lane scope (RQ-03) flips with `"lane": "any"` on its trigger.
- **M4 edge cases.** Meepo's Poof never targets his own lane, so two Meepos in one lane cannot Poof (Valve: "an allied Meepo's lane"). Wrath of Gold repeats at most about 1,000 times before the 2,048-op command budget stops it with `rules_overflow` (Valve has no cap; a player's gold is normally far below 100). Bolt of Damocles and Arcane Assault do not carry damage past a falling tower into its ancient (the tower rule above). When both players lose their second tower at once (Rolling Storm), the match is a draw.
- **M5 edge cases.** Ravenhook's active can be used with nothing to take (no blocker, a creep, or a hero without items): it does nothing and goes on cooldown, as other actives without a choice do (J'Muy with an empty deck). March of the Machines stays in play at 0 charges and does nothing (Valve's text never removes it). When a Chain Frost link dies and its position collapses (nothing stands across it), its two outer neighbors become neighbors and the chain may bounce between them (compaction above). Murder Plot offers combat targets from the board before its +8 Attack, which cannot change positions. Heartstopper Aura and Coordinated Assault may target an enemy black hero ("a black hero"), whose granted text then works for its owner. A Dirty Deeds or Lodestone Demolition that deals 0 still logs a 0-damage tower hit.
- **M6 edge cases.** Item actives are used from the active lane by an equipped hero there, cost no mana, pass initiative, and are unavailable while the hero is silenced or stunned (glossary) or in the Fountain (RQ-38); cooldowns belong to the item instance. Actives with nothing to act on can still be used and go on cooldown (Nyctasha's Guard with no enemy neighbors, Demagicking Maul with no enemy improvement, Book of the Dead at 0 charges), as Ravenhook's can. Book of the Dead keeps its charges while its hero is in the Fountain (medium confidence; Valve's text is silent). Stonehall growth is granted text on the item: it stacks, stays in the Fountain, survives the opponent's Purge (RQ-12) and is lost when the item is replaced or condemned; a fresh copy starts from its printed bonus. Seraphim Shield lowers the equipped hero's own Attack too ("all units"). Shiva's Guard on an allied target can lower the equipped hero's Attack. Two Shop Deeds make a Secret Shop item pay its base cost (RQ-26); the log reads "is paid" instead of "spends". Golden Ticket's gained card is hidden from the opponent, who learns only that a card was gained.

- **M7 edge cases.** Improvement actives are used from the improvement's chip while its lane is the active lane; they cost no mana, need no hero and pass initiative, and an improvement played this round can use its active at once (RQ-24). Actives with nothing to act on can still be used and go on cooldown (Aghanim's Sanctum at full Mana, Revtel Investments at 0 charges: no gold, still condemned). Revtel Investments has no charges field in Valve's data, so it enters with 0 and gains one after its own lane's combat. Bitter Enemies loses a charge after each of its lane's combats and, at 0, deals 6 damage (tower Armor reduces it) to both towers of its lane before every action phase of that lane; both players losing their second tower to it at once is a draw. Unearthed Secrets counts any damage dealt to your tower in its lane this round, from any source; damage that tower Armor fully absorbs is not dealt. The Oath forbids its owner's spells and creeps while its lane is active, a Cross Lane spell cast from there at another lane included; items and improvements stay legal, and the opponent is unaffected. Glyph of Confusion's stun is its owner's effect, so the other player's Purge (Aphotic Shield, Cleansing Rite) can remove it from their own units (RQ-12). Selemene's Favor and The Tyler Estate move current Mana with the maximum (RQ-52). Watchtower draws only for enemy improvements that enter its lane after it, not for those already there or its own side's. Fractured Timeline's Lock ends with the round (RQ-14), and it may pick a card that is already locked. Homefield Advantage may pick an enemy that is already disarmed or stunned.

## Explicitly missing

No Classic card is missing since M7: all 303 counted cards are in the pack, and only Chain Frost (RQ-16), Enough Magic! (RQ-20) and Kanna (RQ-22) stay drafts until a reference match. Still missing: Secret Shop tiers, if Classic had any (RQ-59); custom deck editing and full constructed/draft modes; timers, spectators, replay playback and content migration.

Never add an unsupported original card to a playable deck with a partial effect. Record it as a `draft` until its complete text and interactions are implemented with tests and a reference (`docs/EFFECTS.md`).
